Alikhan Bokeikhanov

About Alikhan Bokeikhanov

Leader of the Alash movement

Alikhan Bokeikhanov (1866–1937) was a prominent Kazakh statesman, politician, publicist, and intellectual. He dedicated his life to the struggle for the independence and cultural renaissance of the Kazakh people. He was the founder and key leader of the Alash party and served as the Prime Minister of the Alash Autonomy.

Important Events

  1. 1866: Born in the Tokrauyn volost of the Semipalatinsk region.
  2. 1894: Graduated from the Imperial Forestry Institute in St. Petersburg.
  3. 1917: Founded the Alash party and became the head of the Alash Autonomy.
  4. 1937: Tragic death during the political repressions.

Key Contributions

  • Led the national liberation movement of the Kazakh people.
  • Co-founder and editor of the influential "Qazaq" newspaper.
  • Wrote numerous political, historical, and scientific articles.
  • Defended the land rights and promoted modern education for Kazakhs.
